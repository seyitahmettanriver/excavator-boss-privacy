Excavator Boss is an offline, single-player excavator and construction simulator. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal information. The game has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking services, and it does not send any data over the internet.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings only on your own device, for example:

  • money, machines and finished jobs
  • language and graphics options
  • the company name you type in

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with Reset progress in the settings, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The game may use vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
