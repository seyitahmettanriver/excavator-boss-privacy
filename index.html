Excavator Boss – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Excavator Boss ("the game") is an offline single-player excavator and construction simulator. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking services, and it does not send any data over the internet.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (for example money, machines, finished jobs, language, graphics options and the company name you type in) only on your own device. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with "Reset progress" in the settings, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The game may use vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Son güncelleme: 26 Kasım 2026

Cihazınızda saklanan bilgiler

Ayarlardaki "İlerlemeyi sıfırla" ile, uygulama verilerini temizleyerek ya da oyunu kaldırarak istediğiniz zaman silebilirsiniz.
